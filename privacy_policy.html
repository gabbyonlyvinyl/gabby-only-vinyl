Privacy Policy for Gabby Only Vinyl

Last updated: October 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Gabby Only Vinyl mobile application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information processed through the app.

1. Information We Collect

The app does not require account creation for public users and does not collect personal identity information such as your name, address, or payment details.

  • Push Notification Tokens: If you allow push notifications, an anonymous device token is generated via Firebase Cloud Messaging solely to deliver updates regarding tour dates, news, and sets.
  • Usage Analytics: Standard anonymous diagnostic data may be processed to ensure app stability and performance.

2. Third-Party Services

The app utilizes trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify your device:

  • Google Play Services & Firebase Cloud Messaging (Push Notifications)
  • Supabase (Content delivery for events, gallery, and news)

3. Data Sharing & Selling

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via the official channels of DJ Gabby.
